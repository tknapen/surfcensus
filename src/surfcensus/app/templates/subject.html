Subject

quality control of the raw data and of the preprocessing, session by session

The EPI stream of a participant plays every packed run, session after session, as one moving picture: the raw series with nothing done to it but one rigid placement per session in the anatomy (that of the anchor run's registration), for quality control of the raw data (motion, artefacts in single volumes, distortion and dropout as acquired, movement between runs); and the series as the pipeline wrote it, for quality control of the preprocessing (what was corrected, what remains, how it lies on the white and pial contours). The state of a run is what the catalog records under the present pack settings.

Subject

quality control of the raw data and of the preprocessing, session by session

The EPI stream of a participant plays every packed run, session after session, as one moving picture. {{ streams }} The raw stream is there for quality control of the raw data (motion, artefacts in single volumes, distortion and dropout as acquired, movement between runs); the preprocessed one for quality control of the preprocessing (what was corrected, what remains, how it lies on the white and pial contours). The state of a run is what the catalog records under the present pack settings.

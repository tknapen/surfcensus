# Decisions

Log of design decisions. Settled ones record why; open ones record what decides them. Add an entry whenever a choice would be hard to reverse.

## Settled

D1. Local FreeSurfer directories are a first-class source. The census is one use of the package, not its only one. Consequence: everything downstream of `Source` sees local paths only; `surfcensus review PATH` must work offline in one command.

D2. Default renderer is pure Python (nibabel + matplotlib), Freeview is optional. Reason: headless, deterministic, no FreeSurfer license, parallel. Freeview stays for figures and as a cross-check.

D3. Three planes around the occipital pole, ping-pong, with frame-level scrubbing. Reason: Tomas's specification; scrubbing because experienced raters work frame by frame.

D4. Cortical thickness is the quantitative anchor, scored per vertex in fsaverage space (fsaverage6 default) against a per-vertex reference distribution. Parcel means are kept as a cheap baseline. Reason: the failure is local, and normal thickness varies over short distances in occipital cortex, so only a location-matched reference can say what is abnormal; fsaverage also puts rater marks and metrics on the same vertices. Supersedes the earlier plan of per-subject direction-of-normal ROIs, which remain only as an optional cross-check.

D8. Raters mark errors by clicking, and marks are resolved to native and fsaverage6 vertices. Required for positive ratings in census mode, optional locally. Reason: turns per-subject ratings into spatial ground truth, which enables census error maps, vertex-level detection analysis, and a test of whether the metric flags the right place.

D10. Datasets are shown by pseudonym by default, in the app, gallery, figures, tables and released data. Reason: to keep goodwill with the labs that shared data; the paper's point is about practice in the field, not about individual groups. Implementation:

- The pseudonym is a keyed hash: the first 8 hex characters of HMAC-SHA256(key, "<source>:<accession>"), prefixed `D-` (e.g. `D-3f9a1c07`). A plain unkeyed hash would be worthless: OpenNeuro accessions are a small enumerable set, so anyone could hash all of them and invert the mapping in seconds.
- The key is 32 random bytes generated by `surfcensus key init`, stored outside the run directory and outside version control (default `~/.config/surfcensus/dataset.key`, path configurable). Run directories record only the key's fingerprint (SHA-256 of the key, first 12 hex), so outputs made with different keys cannot be mixed by accident.
- The catalog stores accessions (it must, to fetch data), but no exported artefact does. `surfcensus export` writes pseudonyms only; `surfcensus export --reveal` writes the mapping table and requires the key. Collisions among 8-hex pseudonyms are checked at discovery and resolved by lengthening the affected pseudonyms.
- Reviewers or editors who need the mapping get it confidentially.

What pseudonymization does not do: it is goodwill, not secrecy. Per-dataset subject counts, field strength, age ranges and the renders themselves are derived from public data and can, with effort, be matched to OpenNeuro metadata. Released per-dataset tables therefore report coarse descriptors (field strength, subject count binned) rather than exact acquisition details. The paper should state plainly that identities are withheld as a courtesy, not hidden.

D9. The per-vertex reference is built twice: first from all subjects, then from rater-passed subjects. Reason: common failures contaminate the reference exactly where they occur; reporting both measures that cost.

D5. OpenNeuroDerivatives and embedded derivatives are analysed separately. Reason: one pipeline versus heterogeneous pipelines answer different questions.

D6. Raters are blind to dataset, scores and other ratings during rating. Local mode may sort by score unless `--blind`.

D7. The frontend has no build step. Reason: pip-installable without Node; easy to maintain.

D11 (2026-11-12). Render output is per-panel slice tiles in two layers, composed in the browser: a lossy grayscale WebP of the T1 slice and a lossless RGBA WebP of the contours. The browser stacks the layers, places the three panels, draws crosshairs and orientation letters, and plays both playback modes from frame tables in `render.json`. `anim.mp4` and `poster.webp` remain composite three-panel strips. Reason: lossy WebP at quality 80 desaturates thin coloured lines (mean absolute error 47, 16, 70 of 255 on contour pixels, measured on `ds000102` sub-01); composite strips cost about 10 MB per subject against about 4 MB for layered tiles (estimate from 15 sagittal tiles, one hemisphere); a separate contour layer lets raters hide the contours to see the tissue under the line; and one pixel-to-voxel affine per panel replaces one per frame. Supersedes the composite-strip frames first described in `rendering.md`. Consequence for the protocol: raters can toggle contours, which the pilot should take into account when timing ratings.

D12 (2026-11-12). The occipital box is anchored on the geometric pole (most posterior pial vertex per hemisphere). From milestone 3 the renderer also maps a fixed fsaverage pole vertex to native space through `sphere.reg` and records its distance to the geometric pole in `render.json`. The pilot decides from those distances whether the anchor should change. Reason: the geometric pole needs no template, so single-subject rendering stays self-contained, but it depends on head pitch and on the pial surface being correct at the pole.

D13 (2026-11-12). Panels follow Freeview's default 2D display orientation, with orientation letters on the panel edges, so the phase 0 comparison with Freeview is like for like. Implemented as two config switches (`radiological`, default true: subject's left on the right of the coronal and axial panels; `sagittal_anterior_left`, default false: posterior at the left). `render.json` records the orientation used. The defaults were first set from memory, with anterior at the left of the sagittal panel; reading Freeview's source the same day corrected that. In `RenderView2D.cpp` (dev branch; the sagittal and radiological cases are the same in v6.0.0 and v7.1.1) the sagittal camera sits at +x with view-up +z, which puts anterior at the right; the coronal camera sits at +y and the axial camera at −z with view-up +y, which is radiological with anterior up. A published Freeview screenshot shows the same edge labels. A neurological view exists only as an option (`-neuro-view`, from 7.4.0). The by-eye comparison of milestone 1 remains the check on a rendered frame.

D14 (2026-11-12). The fsaverage6 template (spheres, cortex label, search-region labels) is looked up in this order: a local FreeSurfer install, an `fsaverage/` directory next to the subjects, then a copy bundled in the wheel (about 1 to 2 MB). Reason: D1 requires `surfcensus review` to work offline, which fetch-on-first-use does not. Condition: bundling goes ahead only after Tomas confirms that the FreeSurfer licence permits redistributing these fsaverage-derived files (nilearn and TemplateFlow do so; the terms have not been read for this project).

D15 (2026-11-12; refined the same day in the review of milestone 1). Vertex-level spatial detectability is evaluated, per subject, only on search-region vertices that the subject's tiles show: the vertices with `visible` true in `vertex_map.npz`, whose pial position lies in the clickable region of `rendering.md`. Reason: raters can only mark what a tile shows, so search-region vertices that no tile shows would count as negatives that nobody inspected. The first wording was "inside that subject's rendered box (the vertices in `vertex_map.npz`)". It treated three sets as one: the box, what the tiles show (10 mm more than the box in x and z and 13.5 mm more in y with the default config), and the contents of the file, which now also holds a margin of vertices beyond what is shown, so that marks near a tile edge resolve to the right vertex. On `ds000102` sub-01 the box holds 31,548 pial vertices, 55,534 are visible, and the file holds 74,654. The parcel-based search region is kept for scoring.

D16 (2026-11-12). When no shipped reference applies and the local set has fewer than 30 subjects, no z-maps are computed; subjects are ranked on the raw ceiling fraction and the raw occipital-to-control regional thickness ratio, and the output says so. Reason: a per-vertex median and MAD from a handful of subjects is not meaningful. Whether to ship a provisional reference built from unrated census subjects stays open under O7.

D17 (2026-11-12). The code is licensed BSD-3-Clause. The licence for released ratings stays open under O12.

D18 (2026-11-12). Working conventions. Geometry is in mm throughout, so volumes that are not 1 mm conformed are handled. The T1 is upsampled by nearest neighbour, as in Freeview. "Deterministic" (hard rule in `CLAUDE.md`) means for a given input, config and locked environment (`uv.lock`); golden tests use a tolerance. The catalog uses stdlib `sqlite3` with `PRAGMA user_version` migrations, and `sqlmodel` stays in the `app` extra. The version stays static until the first tagged release. Regional metrics, ceiling fraction and scoring are built in milestone 3 because `review` orders its queue by the composite score; the intensity metric follows milestone 4. CI runs lint and tests on Linux and macOS for Python 3.11 and 3.13.

D19 (2026-11-12). Choices made while building milestone 1, with their reasons.

- Orientation check. nibabel's vox2ras-tkr ignores the direction cosines, so the documented test (tkr matrix not a signed permutation) could never fail on a file. The renderer refuses a volume whose scanner affine and tkr affine differ in their linear part, that is, any volume not in conformed LIA orientation.
- Contours are joined into polylines before drawing (see `rendering.md`); `LineCollection` of separate segments gave unevenly heavy lines.
- The video setting is part of a render's identity: asking for a render without video in a directory that has one renders again and removes the video.
- `vertex_map.npz` is stored uncompressed with fixed entry dates, for byte identity across zlib builds (1.8 MB instead of about 1.3 MB on one subject; with the selection rule of the coordinates review below, 2.5 MB instead of 1.7 MB from `numpy.savez_compressed`).
- The video is converted with the BT.709 matrix, limited range, and tagged, so players need not guess; swscale's bit-exact path is forced because its SIMD path gave different bytes.
- `three_plane(subject, "x.mp4")` renders into a hidden temporary directory next to the target and moves the video into place.
- `render.json` records an input only under a name that FreeSurfer gives a file of that role. A link to a pial variant in the same directory (`lh.pial -> lh.pial.T1`, FreeSurfer 7) is recorded under the variant's name. A link to anything else, in the same or in another directory, is recorded under the link's own name, and a file handed to `render_subject` under a name that is not the role's (a download cache may do that) under the role's first name. So no foreign name reaches `render.json`. Before the review a link such as `T1.mgz -> ds000999_sub-01_T1.mgz` in the same directory put the target's name into `render.json`.
- A directory is treated as an fsaverage template if its own name or the name of its link target starts with `fsaverage`.
- Tests run the package under jaxtyping and beartype with the PEP 484 numeric tower (an `int` passes where `float` is annotated), in agreement with the static checker.
- Byte identity of renders was checked on one machine across Python 3.11 (numpy 2.4) and 3.13 (numpy 2.5): identical media bytes. Not checked across machines or operating systems.

Added in the review of milestone 1 (robustness, 2026-11-12):

- Renders into one media directory are serialized by `flock` on the directory itself. Chosen over a lock file: it leaves nothing in the directory that the completeness check, the cleanup or the list of a render's files would have to know about, and the kernel releases it when the process dies, so a killed render cannot leave the directory locked. A lock file made with `O_EXCL` would be portable, but it stays behind after a kill and then needs a rule for when it is stale. Where `flock` is missing (Windows) or refused, the render runs without a lock, as it did before. Measurements and what was not checked (macOS, NFS) are in `rendering.md`.
- Cleanup removes files by the names a render writes, not by glob over the directory: a user's `.notes.tmp` or `tiles/mine.webp` in the output directory used to be deleted. `anim.mp4` is such a name and is still removed by a render without video, also when the output directory is one the user keeps other things in. Temporary files are removed only under the lock.
- Rendering into the subject directory itself is not refused. It adds files to a source directory, which the hard rule on local sources argues against; whether `three_plane` should refuse an output inside the subject directory is left to milestone 2, where the run directory is the only output location.
- `RenderConfig` checks colours (`#RRGGBB`) and bounds `line_width_px` by `panel_px / 8`, and `render_subject` validates the config again, since `model_copy(update=...)` skips validation. Before, a bad colour or a very wide line failed while drawing, after `render.json` of a complete render had been removed. The bound of an eighth is a choice: 40 px at the default panel, 2 px at the smallest (16 px), where the default line of 1.25 px still passes.
- Absent and unreadable are told apart by the package's own `stat()` probe (`sources/patterns.py`), not by `Path.is_file()`: for a file in a directory without search permission, `is_file()` raises `PermissionError` on Python 3.11.18 and 3.13.18 and returns False on 3.14.6 (run 2026-11-12 on Linux without `CAP_DAC_OVERRIDE`). `resolve_subject_files` and `is_freesurfer_subject_dir` raise `OSError` for a path they cannot examine, and the renderer turns that into `render failure`. They do not answer "absent" or "not a subject", because a scan would then drop an unreadable subject without a trace. The scan of milestone 2 has to catch the error for each directory and record it as unreadable. A name too long for the file system counts as absent.
- With a video target, `three_plane` does every check before it creates the parent directory or the temporary directory, and removes the directories it created if the render fails afterwards. The command line handles SIGTERM like Ctrl-C; a render killed with SIGKILL still leaves its hidden temporary directory next to the target.

Added in the review of milestone 1 (determinism, 2026-11-12):

- The contour renderer resets `path.sketch` on its artists, so a `matplotlibrc` or an earlier `plt.xkcd()` cannot change a tile. Chosen over drawing inside `matplotlib.rc_context` with default settings: that would change global state for the length of a draw, which is not safe with two threads rendering in one process, and the review's sweep of all rcParams found no other setting that reaches the tile. Measurements are in `rendering.md`.
- Config colours have one validation, a field validator that requires `#RRGGBB` and stores the upper-case form; it replaces the pattern check added in the robustness review. Lower-case colours used to give another config hash for the same bytes, so a change of letter case in a run config would have rendered every subject again. The hash of the default config did not change (`dff40fe66869`): the defaults were already upper case, and a validation rule is not part of the hashed payload. A test pins that value, since it may change only when the serialized value of a default or the schema version changes.
- `vox2pix` is the inverse of `pix2vox` in closed form, with negative zeros removed from both matrices. `np.linalg.inv` gave the last bit of the shifts and a `-0.0` of the LAPACK that numpy is linked with, which `uv.lock` does not pin (the review notes OpenBLAS in the Linux wheel and Accelerate in the macOS arm64 wheel, and reproduced other digits with reference LAPACK 3.12 on Linux). Checked on this machine on 10 renders before and after the change (synthetic subjects at 1, 0.8 and 0.7 mm and `ds000102` sub-01, panels of 64 to 384 px, both display orientations): every tile, poster, video and vertex map is byte-identical, and `render.json` differs only in `vox2pix`: `-0.0` became `0.0` in every render (one entry in the axial panel, four entries with the flipped orientations), and the last digit of a shift changed in 3 of the 10 (0.8 mm at the default panel, sub-01 at 384 px and at 334 px; for example `-160.0` became `-160.00000000000003`, the value the review got from reference LAPACK). Not removed: contour coordinates still pass through `np.linalg.inv` of the vox2ras-tkr affine (`geometry.tkr_to_pixel`), so tile bytes could in principle depend on LAPACK in the last bit of a coordinate. The review rendered three subjects with reference LAPACK and found all media files identical. Not checked on macOS.
- When the video is asked for, the ffmpeg binary is looked up before the volume and the surfaces are loaded and before anything is written, and a missing one is a `RenderError` with reason `render failure`. Before, `IMAGEIO_FFMPEG_EXE` pointing at a missing file surfaced as `FileNotFoundError` after `render.json` of a complete render had been removed and all tiles written again. The reason is one of the protocol's unratable reasons, although the cause lies in the environment and not in the subject: the census render phase (milestone 5) must not record it as a property of the subject. `write_mp4` now raises `RuntimeError`, as its documentation says, also for a binary that cannot be started and for one that exits with status 0 without a video. Both used to raise `OSError`, and a binary that exited with status 0 and left an empty file would have replaced an existing `anim.mp4` with it.
- The command-line tests do not depend on the terminal. Typer hands usage errors and help pages to Rich and forces Rich's terminal output when `GITHUB_ACTIONS`, `FORCE_COLOR` or `PY_COLORS` is set; Rich then puts escape codes inside option names (`--no-video` in three pieces), and it wraps to the terminal width. At the reviewed commit that failed `test_no_video_contradicts_a_video_path`, in every cell of the CI matrix according to the review; the test of an empty `--out` added in the robustness review failed the same way (run here with `GITHUB_ACTIONS=true` and `FORCE_COLOR=1` before the change: 3 failed, 9 passed in `tests/test_cli_render.py`). The tests now read the output with escape codes and frame removed and white space collapsed, and each test that reads such output runs three times (plain, forced colour at 80 columns, forced colour at 30 columns) with the variables Rich reads set by the fixture, so the CI condition is tested in every environment. Not chosen: disabling Rich for the tests (`_TYPER_FORCE_DISABLE_TERMINAL`), which would leave the output users get on CI untested.

Added in the review of milestone 1 (coordinates, 2026-11-12):

- `vertex_map.npz` is selected by what the tiles show, not by the view box, and D15 is refined accordingly. The rule, the measurements on `ds000102` sub-01 and the cost (1.8 to 2.5 MB for the file, 10.2 to 10.9 MB of media per subject) are in `rendering.md`.
- The region is the union of the three panels' boxes, not the cube of the field of view. The cube is simpler and nearly the same size (with a 5 mm margin 75,435 vertices on sub-01 against 74,654), but its eight corner blocks are shown by no tile: they lie outside the slice range of all three panels. 602 pial vertices of sub-01 lie there, and `visible` must be false for them, or D15 would count them as inspected.
- The region is grown box by box along the axes, not by Euclidean distance. That keeps a few vertices near the edges and corners of the grown boxes that no mark can reach, and it lets a reader recompute the selection from `render.json` with comparisons alone.
- Half a slice step means half of `slice_step_mm`, also where the slice planes are farther apart or closer than the step because the voxels are not the size of the step. It matters only at the two ends of each panel's sweep.
- `vertex_map_margin_mm` keeps its name and changes its meaning: the margin beyond the clickable region (default 5 mm, the snap radius) instead of the margin beyond the box (default 10 mm). The file gained arrays and `render.json` gained the counts of visible vertices, so the render schema version went from 1 to 2. The version is part of every config hash, so a render made under the old meaning cannot pass for an up-to-date render of a config that sets the margin to 10, which would otherwise hash like the old default. (The new counts are required fields, so an old `render.json` also fails to load; the version says why.) The hash of the default config changed from `dff40fe66869` to `17f83a0c9b6c`, and the test that pins it was updated. Every existing render is rendered again on the next request; a test does that with a render.json and vertex map in the form of version 1.
- `vertexmap.build_vertex_map` takes the region as an array of boxes instead of the view box, and `read_vertex_map` returns the `visible` arrays with the rest.
- `visible` is defined on the pial position alone. A vertex whose white position is shown and whose pial position is not is in the file and is not visible. D15 concerns pial capture; if white-surface marks (class C) get their own spatial analysis, it needs its own flag.
- Not done here: the app (milestone 4) has to refuse a snap radius larger than the `vertex_map_margin_mm` of the render it serves. Measured on one real subject (FreeSurfer 6.0.1, 1 mm) and on synthetic subjects at 1 and 0.8 mm; no FreeSurfer 7 subject.

## Open

O1. Package name. `surfcensus` is a placeholder. Decide with the paper team.

O2. When and how to reveal dataset identities. D10 settles the default (pseudonyms). Open: whether to reveal at all, and if so whether after notifying dataset authors, after publication, or on request only. Decide before phase 8.

O12. Licence for released ratings (CC0 proposed in `phases.md`). The code licence is settled in D17.

O3. How over-maximum thickness is stored (clamped or zeroed). Decided by phase 0 inspection; changes metric definitions. Evidence so far (2026-11-12): clamped at 5.0 in one FreeSurfer 6.0.1 subject, and clamped in the source of `MRISmeasureCorticalThickness` at v6.0.0, v7.0.0 and v7.4.1 (see `metrics.md`). No FreeSurfer 7 data inspected yet.

O4. Whether the view box scales with brain size. Decided by pilot renders on atypical brains.

O5. fsaverage resolution: fsaverage6 (40,962 vertices per hemisphere) or full fsaverage (163,842). Decided in pilot by whether fsaverage6 resolves the smallest marked errors.

O10. Which fsaverage sphere is canonical when several are available (local FreeSurfer, the copy inside each fMRIPrep derivative, nilearn). Default: the copy matching the subject's FreeSurfer version. Requires checking that the copies agree and that nilearn's sphere uses FreeSurfer's convention.

O11. Cluster threshold (z > 3 default) and the area measure for clusters (subject's resampled vertex areas or fsaverage areas).

O6. Number of raters per subject (2 or 3) and rater pool. Decided from pilot agreement and time per subject.

O7. Whether the normative reference ships in the first release or waits for census ratings. Default: ships empty, populated in the release after the census. Option raised 2026-11-12: ship a provisional first-pass reference built from a few hundred unrated OpenNeuroDerivatives subjects, labelled as such (see D16).

O8. Hosting for the team app and media (lab server at the Spinoza Centre or VU, or a cloud VM and bucket).

O9. Whether to include the embedded-derivatives source in the first paper or defer it. It is more work (heterogeneous paths) and answers a secondary question.
